Refund and Returns Policy

How cancellations and refunds work for Made Well’s online services. Last updated: 26 November 2026.

1. About this policy

This policy applies to what you buy from Made Well Media (“Made Well”, “we”, “us”) on this website: the Made Well Pro subscription, company registration, and beneficial ownership filing. It is read together with our Terms and Conditions and is meant to be consistent with the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (CPA) and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act 25 of 2002 (ECTA). Nothing in this policy limits your rights under those laws.

2. Services, not goods

We sell online services and digital content. We do not ship physical goods, so there is nothing to return. Instead, this policy explains when you can cancel and when you will be refunded.

3. 7-day cooling-off period

If you buy from us online as a consumer, you may cancel an order within 7 days of the agreement being concluded, without giving a reason and without a penalty, except in cases the law excludes from the cooling-off right. To cancel, contact us as set out in section 9.

If we have not yet started work on your order when you cancel, you will receive a full refund. If we have already started (for example we have submitted your company registration to the CIPC), we will refund what is left after deducting official fees we have already paid on your behalf and work already done at your request.

4. Company registration and beneficial ownership filing

  • Before we start: if you cancel before we begin processing your order, we refund the full amount.
  • After we start: official fees already paid to the CIPC or another authority cannot be recovered, so they are not refunded. Any remaining balance is refunded.
  • Name not approved: if the CIPC does not approve the company names you gave us, we will ask you for alternative names and resubmit at no extra charge.
  • If we cannot complete your order because of a mistake on our side, we will correct it at no cost or refund you in full.
  • If we cannot complete your order because information or documents you gave us were incorrect or missing and you choose not to continue, we refund the amount less official fees already paid.
  • After delivery: once the registration or filing documents have been delivered to you, the service has been performed and is not refundable.

5. Made Well Pro subscription (R199 per month)

  • Cancel any time from your dashboard. There is no contract and no cancellation fee.
  • When it stops: cancelling stops future monthly payments. You keep Pro until the end of the month you have already paid for. We do not refund part-months.
  • First payment: if you change your mind within 7 days of your first payment, contact us and we will refund that payment, provided you have not used Pro-only features (more than one download, or emailing documents) in that time.
  • Failed payments: if a monthly payment fails, your Pro access may pause until payment succeeds.
  • Free plan: the free plan costs nothing and can be used or closed at any time.

6. Promo codes

Promo codes give free access to Pro for a period or without an end date. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.

7. Mistakes and double payments

If you were charged twice, or charged an amount that does not match your order, we will refund the difference in full once we have confirmed it.

8. If the service was not done properly

You have the right to expect services to be performed with reasonable care and skill. If a service of ours falls short, tell us. We will fix it, or if we cannot, refund you as the CPA requires.

9. How to ask for a refund

Message us on WhatsApp 067 258 1319 and tell us your name, the email address you used, what you bought, the date, and (if you have it) your PayFast payment reference.

We aim to reply within 2 business days.

10. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method through PayFast. This normally takes 7 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version on this page when you place an order is the one that applies to that order.
