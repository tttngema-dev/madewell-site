Questions, answered

Is browsing tenders really free?

Yes. You can search every open tender and preview its documents without an account. Your first download is free too. Unlimited downloads are part of Pro.

What do I get when I create a free account?

You can save tenders and track closing dates, keep your company profile, documents and signature ready, and fill in, attach to and send your first tender document completely free.

What does Pro add?

Pro is R199 a month. It lets you fill in unlimited documents, with your stamp, signature and attached company documents, email them straight to the buyer, and get daily tender alerts matched to your business.

Where do the tenders come from?

From official South African public tender sources. Always confirm the closing time and the official bid pack before you bid.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, any time from your dashboard. You keep Pro until the end of the month you have paid for, and everything you saved stays in your account.

Is the receipts and invoices app free?

Yes. It is free to use, needs no account, and works on your phone even offline. Open it from Business Tools.

Do you also register companies?

Yes. Company registration (R580) and beneficial ownership filing (R500) are on our Business Services page.
