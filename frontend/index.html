Questions, answered

How long does registration take?

Registrations are commonly completed within a few business days, but the timing depends on CIPC. We’ll email you as soon as your documents are ready.

What does R780 cover?

It’s our fixed price to prepare and submit your private company registration. We’ll confirm exactly what’s included in your confirmation email.

Can I register without a name?

Yes. Tick the option on the first step and we’ll register your company with a number. You can choose a name later.

Is my personal information safe?

We only collect what CIPC needs to register your company. Your details are stored securely and used only for this registration.

What happens after I pay?

We email you to collect certified copies of each director’s ID. Once we have them, we submit your registration to CIPC.
