Who buys in Eastern Cape

Tenders for Eastern Cape are advertised by metropolitan municipalities including Nelson Mandela Bay (Gqeberha) and Buffalo City (East London), district municipalities such as OR Tambo, Amathole, Chris Hani, Alfred Nzo, Joe Gqabi and Sarah Baartman, provincial departments (for example Health, Education, Public Works and Transport), and national public entities that work in the province, such as SANRAL, Transnet and Eskom.

The provincial capital is Bhisho, and other main centres include Gqeberha, East London, Mthatha and Makhanda. If you only work in part of Eastern Cape, use the search box on the tenders page to narrow results by town, municipality or department.

Who advertises tenders in Eastern Cape?

Buyers in Eastern Cape include metropolitan municipalities including Nelson Mandela Bay (Gqeberha) and Buffalo City (East London), district municipalities such as OR Tambo, Amathole, Chris Hani, Alfred Nzo, Joe Gqabi and Sarah Baartman, provincial departments such as Health, Education, Public Works and Transport, and national public entities with offices in the province.

Do I need to be registered to bid for tenders in Eastern Cape?

Usually yes. Most buyers ask for a Central Supplier Database (CSD) registration, a valid tax compliance status and your company documents. Each tender lists exactly what it needs.
