Make professional receipts and invoices in seconds, and send them to your customer straight from your phone.

Good to know

Because your documents are kept on your device and not on our servers:

  • Back them up now and then: Options → Back up your data
  • Clearing your browser data, or losing your phone, removes them unless you have a backup
  • Your documents on one phone do not appear on another
How to put it on your home screen

iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android (Chrome): tap the menu (three dots), then Install app or Add to Home screen.
