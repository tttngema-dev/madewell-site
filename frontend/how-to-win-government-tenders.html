How to win government tenders: 10 practical tips

Winning tenders is mostly about preparation and compliance. These are the habits that separate bids that get scored from bids that get thrown out.

  1. Be registered and compliant before you look. CSD, tax status, B-BBEE proof and company documents ready. See CSD registration.
  2. Choose tenders you can actually deliver. Match the scope, the location and the size to your capacity.
  3. Read the whole document. Closing time, place of submission, mandatory documents and evaluation criteria are all in there.
  4. Attend the briefing. If it is compulsory and you miss it, you are out.
  5. Ask questions early. Send queries in writing before the deadline the buyer sets.
  6. Price the full job. Include labour, materials, transport, insurance and the whole contract period.
  7. Fill in every form completely. Leave nothing blank, sign where asked, and use the original forms. Our checklist helps.
  8. Claim points you can prove. Read how preference points work.
  9. Submit early. Late bids, wrong addresses and unreadable files are the most avoidable losses.
  10. Keep a record. Save every submission and learn from the results. Over time your bids get faster and better.
