Company registration · R580

How registration works

  1. Tell us your details

    Company name, directors, owners and addresses. It takes about ten minutes.

  2. Pay securely

    Pay the R580 fee online through PayFast.

  3. We register you

    We reserve your name and submit everything to CIPC for you.

  4. Receive your documents

    Your registration documents arrive in your inbox.

What you’ll need

Have these ready and the form takes minutes.

  • Company names

    Up to four choices, in order of preference.

  • Director details

    Full name, ID or passport number, home address, email and mobile.

  • Ownership split

    Who owns the company, and what percentage each person holds.

  • Business addresses

    Registered office, postal address, and a company email and phone.

  • Certified ID copies

    After you pay, we’ll email you to collect these for each director.

Questions, answered

How long does registration take?

Registration takes up to 3 business days. We’ll email you as soon as your documents are ready.

What does R580 cover?

It’s our fixed price to prepare and submit your private company registration. We’ll confirm exactly what’s included in your confirmation email.

Can I register without a name?

Yes. Tick the option on the first step and we’ll register your company with a number. You can choose a name later.

Is my personal information safe?

We only collect what CIPC needs to register your company. Your details are stored securely and used only for this registration.

What happens after I pay?

We email you to collect certified copies of each director’s ID. Once we have them, we submit your registration to CIPC.
