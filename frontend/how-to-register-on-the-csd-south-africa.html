How to register on the Central Supplier Database (CSD)

To do business with government you normally need to be registered on the Central Supplier Database, run by National Treasury. Registration is free.

What is the CSD?

The Central Supplier Database is the national list of suppliers that government buyers use. When you register, you receive a supplier number (often called a MAAA number) and a CSD report that most tenders ask you to submit.

What you usually need

  • A registered company, close corporation or business, or you as a sole proprietor, with your registration documents. If you do not have a company yet, we can register your company for a fixed R580.
  • A valid tax compliance status with SARS.
  • A bank confirmation letter for your business bank account.
  • Your B-BBEE certificate or sworn affidavit, if you claim preference points.
  • Identity documents for the owners and directors.

The steps

  1. Go to the CSD website run by National Treasury and create a supplier profile with your email address.
  2. Enter your business details and upload the documents it asks for.
  3. Wait for the system to verify your details with the other government databases (for example CIPC and SARS).
  4. Download your CSD report once your profile is verified, and keep it updated.

Verification can fail when details do not match, for example a company name spelled differently from CIPC records. Fix the mismatch at the source and update your profile.

Keep it up to date

Your tax status and other details expire or change, and buyers check them when they evaluate your bid. Review your profile regularly. Rules and screens change, so always follow what the CSD website currently asks for.
