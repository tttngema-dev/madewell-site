What medical and health tenders usually ask for

  • The correct licences for the products (for example SAHPRA licences for medicines and many medical devices)
  • Tax compliance status and an active CSD registration
  • Manufacturer authorisation, product specifications and sometimes samples
  • Completed SBD forms and a preference points claim (SBD 6.1)

Tip: Check whether the tender is a transversal contract or a once-off supply, and read the delivery and shelf-life requirements carefully.

These are typical requirements, not a promise. Every tender sets its own, so read the bid document before you prepare a bid. See also the tender documents checklist and how to register on the CSD.

What do I need to bid for medical and health tenders?

At minimum a Central Supplier Database registration, a valid tax compliance status and your company documents. Each tender lists the extra requirements it asks for, so always read the bid document.
