POPIA Information

How Made Well looks after your personal information under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Last updated: 20 November 2026.

What POPIA is

POPIA is the South African law that says how organisations must collect, use, store and protect personal information, and gives you rights over your own information.

Who is responsible

Made Well Media (“Made Well”) is the responsible party for the personal information you give us through this website.

What we collect, and why

  • Your account: your email address, so you can sign in and so we can send you what you ask for.
  • Your company profile: company name and registration number, addresses, tax and VAT numbers, directors, and optional banking details you choose to save. Used only to fill in your tender documents for you.
  • Documents and signature you upload: company documents and your signature, kept in private storage and used only when you attach or apply them.
  • Tenders and filled-in documents: the tenders you save and the documents you fill in.
  • Business Tools (receipts and invoices): the receipts, invoices, customers and products you create are stored on your own device, not on our servers.
  • Tender alerts: your preferences and email address, to send the alert emails you switch on.
  • Company registration and beneficial ownership filings: the directors’ and owners’ details and ID copies needed to complete your filing.
  • Payments: PayFast processes your payment and tells us it succeeded. We never see or store your card details.
  • Visit statistics and advertising measurement: anonymous visit data and the Meta Pixel, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Who we share it with

Only with those who help us deliver the service: PayFast for payments, the CIPC for registrations and filings you ask us to make, and the providers that run our website, database and email. They may only use your information to provide their service to us. We do not sell your personal information.

Your rights

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Object to how we use it, or withdraw your consent.
  • Switch off tender alert emails at any time from your dashboard or the link in every email.
  • Complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za if you are unhappy with our response.

How to make a request

Message us on WhatsApp 067 258 1319 from our Contact page and tell us what you would like us to do.

How we protect it

Our database is access-controlled, your uploaded documents are kept in private storage, payments happen on PayFast’s secure pages, and our site uses HTTPS. If a breach ever affects you, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as the law requires.
