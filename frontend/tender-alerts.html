Why alerts win tenders

Most tenders are open for two to four weeks. If you find one with two days left, you rarely have time to prepare a proper bid. Alerts put new tenders in front of you in the first days, when you can still attend the briefing, ask questions and prepare your documents.

What you get with Pro

  • A daily email with the best new tenders for your business, up to ten a day
  • Unlimited tender documents to fill in, with your stamp and signature
  • Your company documents attached and sent as one PDF
  • Unlimited downloads of tender documents

Prefer to look yourself? Browse open tenders or pick your province or category.

Tender alerts: questions

How do tender alerts work?

You tell Made Well what you bid on. Every morning we email you the best new open tenders that match, up to ten a day, each with a link straight to the tender. You never get the same tender twice.

Is it free?

Tender alerts are part of Made Well Pro at R199 a month. Browsing and previewing tenders is free, and your first download and first fill-in document are free.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from your dashboard. You keep Pro until the end of the month you have paid for.
