About Made Well

Business paperwork, made simpler.

Made Well helps South African businesses get the paperwork side of business done, starting with finding and responding to tenders.

Tenders

We bring open South African tenders into one searchable place, with documents you can preview for free. With a free account you can save tenders, keep your company details and documents ready, and fill in your first tender document. Pro adds unlimited documents and daily tender alerts.

Business services

We register private companies (Pty) Ltd with CIPC and file beneficial ownership documents, at fixed prices. See Business Services.

Business tools

A free receipts and invoices app that works on your phone, even offline. More tools are planned. See Business Tools.

Where our tender information comes from

Tender details come from official South African public tender sources. Always confirm the closing time and the official bid pack before you bid.

Who we are

Made Well is run by Made Well Media. We are an independent service provider, not the CIPC or any government body.
