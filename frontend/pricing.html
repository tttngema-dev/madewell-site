Pricing questions

Do I need a card to try it?

No. Browsing, previewing, your first download and your first fill-in document are free, with no payment details needed.

What counts as my free download?

You can download one tender document, once. Previewing every document stays free and unlimited. More downloads are part of Pro.

What counts as my free document?

The first tender document you start filling in. You can keep editing it and attach your company documents. Downloading or emailing the finished PDF is free once. After that, it is part of Pro.

How does paying work?

Pro is R199 a month, charged to your card through PayFast. You can switch it off any time from your dashboard.

What happens if I cancel?

You keep Pro until the end of the month you have paid for. Everything you saved stays in your account. You can go back to the free plan, or restart Pro whenever you like.

What is your refund policy?

You can cancel Pro any time. For refunds, the 7-day cooling-off period and how company registration refunds work, read our Refund Policy.

I have a promo code. Where do I enter it?

Sign in, then use the “Have a promo code?” box at the top of your dashboard.
